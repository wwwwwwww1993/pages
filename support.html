UpInu

单机横屏攀爬游戏。免费，无内购，无广告。

联系：einined@gmail.com

操作

  • 左侧摇杆移动。推过外圈冲刺。
  • 按住「跳」跳得更高，松手会提前结束上升。
  • 空中再点一次开伞滑翔。到了没有空气的高处，同一按键变为喷气。
  • 右侧空白处拖动，转动视角。
  • 走到可抓的边缘会自动抓住。

掉落是正常的

游戏没有检查点。掉下去之后，从落点继续往上爬。进度保存在本机。

回放

暂停菜单可以把最近 15 秒或 30 秒存成视频到相册。游戏只申请添加到相册，不会读取相册里的其他内容。

UpInu

An offline landscape climbing game. Free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

Contact: einined@gmail.com

Controls

  • Move with the left stick. Push past the outer ring to sprint.
  • Hold Jump to jump higher. Release to cut the jump short.
  • Tap again in the air to glide. Where there is no air, the same button is a jet boost.
  • Drag the empty right side to look around.
  • Walk into a grabbable edge and you grab it automatically.

Falling is part of the game

There are no checkpoints. After a fall, keep climbing from wherever you landed. Progress stays on the device.

Replay

The pause menu can save the last 15 or 30 seconds as a video to your photo library. The game only asks to add that video. It does not read the rest of your library.
