UpInu Support

An offline landscape climbing game. Free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

For inquiries, bug reports, or feedback, please contact: einined@gmail.com

Controls

  • Move with the left virtual joystick. Push past the outer ring to sprint.
  • Hold Jump to jump higher; release early to cut the jump short.
  • Tap the jump button again in mid-air to deploy your glider. In vacuum/high altitudes, this button activates a jet boost.
  • Drag anywhere on the right side of the screen to rotate the camera.
  • Move toward any climbable ledge to grab it automatically.

Falling is Part of the Game

There are no checkpoints. If you fall, you resume your climb from wherever you land. All progress and records are saved locally to your device.

Replay Export

The pause menu allows you to save the last 15 or 30 seconds of gameplay as a video to your photo library. The app only requests "Add to Photos" permission to write this file and never reads or browses your existing photos.
