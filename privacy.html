UpInu Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

UpInu is an offline game developed by Wang Ziqing, an independent developer. If you have any questions or feedback, please contact: einined@gmail.com.

Data We Do Not Collect

UpInu does not require user accounts, displays no advertisements, uses no third-party analytics SDKs, and connects to no external servers operated by the developer. We do not collect, process, or transmit your name, contact information, location, device advertising identifiers (IDFA), or gameplay telemetry.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game Progress & Preferences: Your climb progress, statistics, and settings are saved strictly to your device's local sandbox storage. This data is retained only to allow you to resume your game session. Uninstalling or deleting the application will prompt the operating system to permanently remove these files.
  • Replay Export (Add-Only Photos Access): If you choose to save a gameplay replay via the pause menu, the game exports the recorded video directly to your photo library. This requires the system-level "Add to Photos" permission. The game only writes this specific video clip, never accesses or browses existing photos/videos in your library, and never uploads the recorded file to any server.

Children's Privacy

UpInu does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age limit). The game does not collect personal data from any user, nor does it share or sell personal information to third parties.

Changes to This Policy

Should future updates introduce online capabilities, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics, this privacy policy will be updated prior to release, and corresponding declarations will be adjusted in App Store Connect.
