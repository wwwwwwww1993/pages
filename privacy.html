UpInu 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日

「UpInu」是由 【王梓清】 个人开发的单机游戏。联系邮箱：einined@gmail.com。

我们不收集的内容

游戏没有账号，没有广告，没有第三方统计分析，也没有开发者运营的服务器。我们不会接收你的姓名、联系方式、位置、设备广告标识或游玩记录。

只留在你设备上的数据

  • 攀爬进度、统计和设置写在这台设备的本地存储里，用于下次打开时继续。卸载游戏后，系统会删除这些文件。
  • 你在暂停菜单里选择保存回放时，游戏把一段视频写入你的照片图库。这需要系统的「添加到照片」权限。游戏只写入这一段视频，不读取、不浏览你的相册，也不会把视频上传给开发者。

儿童

游戏不针对儿童收集个人信息，也不向任何人出售数据。

政策变更

若以后加入账号、广告、统计或联网功能，会先更新本页，并在 App Store Connect 的隐私回答里同步修改。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

UpInu is an offline game made by 【姓名】, an independent developer. Contact: 【邮箱】.

Data we do not collect

The game has no account, no ads, no third-party analytics, and no developer-operated server. We do not receive your name, contact details, location, advertising identifier, or play history.

Data that stays on your device

  • Climb progress, statistics, and settings are stored locally so you can continue later. The operating system removes them if you delete the game.
  • If you save a replay from the pause menu, the game writes a video to your photo library. This uses add-only photo permission. The game does not read your library and does not upload the video to the developer.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from children and does not sell data to anyone.

Changes

If accounts, ads, analytics, or online features are added later, this page and the App Store privacy answers will be updated first.
